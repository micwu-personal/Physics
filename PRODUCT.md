# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Curious learners, students, educators, and technically minded visitors who want an intuitive path through cosmology, particle physics, atoms, and chemistry. They may arrive without specialist vocabulary and need each interaction to explain what it represents, what evidence supports it, and where a teaching model differs from physical reality.

## Product Purpose

Turn separate interactive physics pages into one coherent, bilingual learning journey: cosmic origins and expansion, the particle era, nuclei and atoms, element formation in stars and explosions, and the high-energy universe. Success means a learner can follow the causal story, pause to explore a concept in depth, manipulate a scientifically defensible model, and leave knowing which visuals are observations, reconstructions, calculations, or schematics.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, wonder-filled, candid. The experience should feel like an excellent science museum exhibit: visually memorable and inviting, but never more confident than the evidence.

## Anti-references

- Disconnected galleries of topic cards with no causal story.
- Spectacle that invents literal-looking particles, atom orbits, reaction mechanisms, colors, scales, or speeds.
- High-level labels that assume the learner already understands detector layers, orbitals, nucleosynthesis, or model limitations.
- Unmarked simulations, artist concepts, false-color images, or data reconstructions presented as photographs.
- Motion that cannot be paused, obscures the initial state, or ignores reduced-motion preferences.

## Design Principles

1. **Follow cause and consequence.** Every chapter answers what existed, what changed, what evidence we have, and what that change made possible next.
2. **Label the epistemic status.** Mark observations, reconstructed data, calculated probability models, and teaching schematics explicitly.
3. **Progressive depth, never hidden basics.** Keep the main story readable, with inline explainers for terms and optional deeper detail at the moment it becomes relevant.
4. **Let interaction test an idea.** Controls should expose constraints and tradeoffs, not merely trigger decorative motion.
5. **Prefer honest uncertainty.** State when a scale is compressed, a geometry is approximate, a mechanism is simplified, or scientific interpretation remains model-dependent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All essential content must work with keyboard and touch input, retain at least 4.5:1 body-text contrast, include bilingual English/Simplified Chinese copy, and provide readable alternatives for color-coded meaning. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`, persist a user override, and ensure paused visuals still communicate a complete static state.
