    What is calculated, what is imagined?

    Raft draft follows Archimedes’ law and includes the 80 kg player. Two 0.25 m³ timber modules total 405 kg loaded, giving 81% immersion. One floats at 97%, but exceeds the mission’s 90% safety limit—not a physical sinking threshold. Waves, steering drag and terrain are illustrative. Capsules follow gravity (9.81 m/s²) from an 11 m/s throw, without auto-aim.

    The enlarged 10 g relay sample uses fixed-pressure calorimetry (no heat losses or container heat capacity). Melting its sensor triggers fictional machinery; it does not melt a whole bridge. Lab molecular motion, distances and atom colors are schematics, not measured trajectories or molecular dynamics. The scale lens is fictional; water vapor is invisible, unlike condensed droplets. New geometry and audio are generated by original local code—not borrowed game/media assets.
