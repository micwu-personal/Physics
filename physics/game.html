    What is calculated, what is imagined?

    Buoyancy readouts use Archimedes’ law with water density 1000 kg/m³. The cube is enlarged, its settling is illustrative, and we omit flow/drag simulation. Phase temperatures and mass fractions use a piecewise calorimetry model; the open-pressure sample has no heat loss or container heat capacity. Molecular motion, distances and oxygen/hydrogen colors are teaching schematics—not molecular dynamics, measured trajectories or visible atoms. The scale lens is fictional; water vapor is invisible, unlike a cloud of condensed droplets.
