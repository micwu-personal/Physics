Three ideas the field is built on.

What the field asks, and which approximations it trusts.

Each guide now spells out the practical questions, scale choices, and simplifying assumptions that make the field work. A useful model is never just an equation; it is also a statement about what gets ignored and why that is acceptable.

A model you can inspect.

Every guide includes one field-specific visual model or diagram. It is labelled so you know whether it represents direct observation, reconstructed evidence, a calculated model, or a teaching schematic, and it states what has been simplified.

What the evidence looks like.

Physics rarely offers a literal photograph. Each visual below is labelled as an observation, a historical document, reconstructed data, a calculated model, or a teaching schematic, so you always know what you are looking at.

Where the standard framework stops.

A working theory is defined as much by its boundaries as by its successes. These are the places where this field's usual description stops being the right one, or where a common summary of it is misleading.

Claim-by-claim paths into the evidence.
