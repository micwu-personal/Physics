What is the Universe made of?

The mix has changed dramatically over cosmic history. Today, only 5% of the universe is ordinary matter.

How big was the universe?

This compares the physical radius, at each epoch, of the patch that became today's observable universe. It is not the size of the whole universe, which may be infinite.

How does it end?

The outcome depends on whether dark energy remains a cosmological constant, evolves, or the vacuum changes.

What we still don't know

Cosmology has spectacular successes — and equally spectacular open problems.
